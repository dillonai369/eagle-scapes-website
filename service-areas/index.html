Seasonal timing in Aurora

Aurora's growing season opens late April, generally after the last 24°F night — that typically falls between April 18 and April 25. The crabgrass pre-emergent window in Aurora is tight: April 5 to April 15 is when we get the best knockdown. On the back end of the year, lake-effect snow can hit Aurora before it reaches Geauga County, so our Aurora plowing routes are dispatched a full storm system earlier than our Auburn or Bainbridge runs. If you want priority on a snow contract, the Aurora list fills first.
