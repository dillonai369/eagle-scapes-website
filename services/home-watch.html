What Is Home Watch?

Your home, looked after like you never left.

A home watch is a scheduled, documented inspection of your home while you're away — snowbirding for the winter, traveling for work, managing a second home or an estate property.

An empty house is where small problems become disasters: a pinhole leak runs for six weeks, a furnace quits during a January cold snap, a storm peels back shingles nobody sees. Our crew visits on your schedule, checks everything inside and out, and sends you a photo report — so a $50 fix never becomes a $50,000 claim.

Many homeowner policies expect an unoccupied home to be checked regularly for full coverage to apply. Documented visits from a professional service help you meet that standard — check your policy for the details.

Frequently Asked

Home watch questions

What is a home watch service?

A home watch is a scheduled visual inspection of your home and property while you're away for weeks or months. Our crew walks the exterior and interior on a set schedule, checks that all systems and amenities are working, catches problems like leaks, storm damage, pests, or break-ins early, and sends you a photo report after every visit.

How often should my home be checked while I'm away?

Most homeowners choose weekly or bi-weekly visits. If you'll be gone through a Northeast Ohio winter, weekly checks are strongly recommended — frozen pipes and roof leaks do their worst damage when nobody notices for weeks. Some insurance policies also expect regular documented checks on an unoccupied home, so it's worth reviewing yours.

What do you check on each visit?

Outside: roofline, gutters, siding, windows, doors, and any signs of storm damage or attempted entry. Inside: signs of water leaks, HVAC running at the right temperature, water heater, plumbing, electrical panel, fridge and freezer, and any signs of pests, mold, or moisture. We also collect mail, packages, and flyers so the home never looks empty.

Do I get proof of every visit?

Yes. After each visit you get a text or email report with photos and notes — what we checked, what we found, and anything that needs attention. If something's wrong, you hear about it the same day with options for how to handle it.

How much does home watch cost?

Plans are custom-quoted based on your home's size and how often you want visits — weekly and bi-weekly plans are most common. Call (216) 516-7730 or request a quote online and we'll put together a simple flat monthly rate.

Can you handle the lawn and snow while I'm gone too?

That's the best part — one call covers everything. The same crew that checks your home can mow the lawn, maintain the beds, and keep the driveway plowed, so your home looks lived-in all season. A maintained, occupied-looking property is one of the best burglary deterrents there is.
